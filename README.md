# Number Town

A maths adventure for Year 5. Make your own character, walk round a little town, go into a building and play games about place value, rounding, adding and subtracting, graphs, factors, multiplying by 10 and area.
Play it at **https://hamzaashour949.github.io/number-town/** (hold your phone sideways).

Everything is pictures you can drag, tap and swipe, and every answer is shown step by step as an animation. Nothing is typed except your name. Nothing is sent anywhere: your character and your stars are saved on your own device.

Made by Hamza Ashour.
